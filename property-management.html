Frequently asked questions

Do you mark up contractor invoices or use in-house maintenance?

No percentage markup. Third-party vendor invoices are passed through at cost and the itemized invoice is attached to your statement. Repairs above your authorization threshold are not started without your approval.

Do I pay a management fee while the property is vacant?

No. Fees are based on collected rent, so there is no management fee on an empty unit.

What is your tenant screening process?

Background check, income verification and previous landlord references, before a lease is offered. We would rather a unit take a little longer to fill than place a tenant who stops paying in month two.

How often do you walk through the property?

Walkthroughs are scheduled and documented with photos, so you have a record of the unit's condition over time and small problems get caught early.

Will I have one point of contact?

Yes. A named local account manager handles your property, and your residents reach the same team. Missed calls get an automatic text and a logged follow-up.

Is switching from my current manager a hassle?

CRPM handles tenant notifications, ledger reconciliation, security deposit audits and lease file transfers so rent keeps coming in during the handoff.
