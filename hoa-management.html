Frequently asked questions

Is switching management companies going to be disruptive for our community?

CRPM handles the transition: homeowner notifications, ledger reconciliation, bank account and reserve transfers, and vendor and maintenance history. Your board approves the plan and we do the moving.

Will our board have one point of contact?

Yes. A named local account manager is assigned to your association. Board members also have their own login to the AppFolio portal for reports and documents between meetings.

Does CRPM manage condominium associations as well as HOAs?

Yes. CRPM manages both condominium associations and homeowners' associations. Board members for either get their own login to the AppFolio portal, and management plans are tailored to each community.

How do we compare HOA management companies in Akron and Cleveland?

Ask how financial reporting works, who facilitates board meetings, how vendors are coordinated, and whether the team is local. CRPM covers all four: clear budgets and reports, board meeting facilitation, preventative maintenance and vendor coordination, and a team based in Fairlawn that knows Northeast Ohio communities.

How much does HOA management cost?

Every association is different, so CRPM prices HOA management through a tailored proposal instead of a one-size-fits-all rate. After an initial conversation about your community and its needs, your board receives a customized management plan to review. That way you know exactly what is included, and what it costs, before you commit.

How will our board stay informed about the association's finances?

Board members get their own login to CRPM's AppFolio portal. CRPM prepares clear, easy-to-read financial reports covering budgets, dues collection, vendor payments, and reserves.

Can CRPM help run our board meetings?

Yes. Board meeting facilitation is part of CRPM's association management. Between meetings, CRPM stays in touch with board members, residents, and vendors, so issues get handled promptly instead of waiting for the next agenda.

Which communities does CRPM manage?

CRPM manages condominium and homeowners' associations across the Akron and Cleveland areas from its office in Fairlawn. Because the team is local, your board works with people who know Northeast Ohio communities and the vendors who serve them.

Does CRPM follow fair housing laws?

Yes. CRPM is committed to equal housing opportunity and supports the principles of the Fair Housing Act.
